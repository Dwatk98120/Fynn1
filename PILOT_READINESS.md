# Pilot Readiness

Before student use:
1. Complete Caregiver Setup in the app.
2. Review storage and network-analysis behavior.
3. Test Offline Mode.
4. Verify pictures, choices, sentence building, and recorded-word playback work without speech analysis.
5. Treat speech interpretations as possibilities.
6. Confirm intended meanings with the student/caregiver when possible.
7. Keep all student data out of GitHub.
8. Test on the actual Android devices used for the pilot.
9. Review applicable consent, privacy, accessibility, and data-retention requirements.

The personalized learning feature learns only from explicit confirmations. It is not a diagnostic or clinical speech profile.
